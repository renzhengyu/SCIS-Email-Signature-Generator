3. How to Set Up Signature in Microsoft Outlook

Follow the steps below for your Outlook version, or refer to official Microsoft documentation.

  1. Click the Copy Signature (Outlook Ready) button above.
  2. Open Outlook Web App in your browser.
  3. Click the Settings (⚙️) icon in the top header.
  4. In the settings menu, go to Account (or Mail) → Signatures (or Compose and reply).
  5. Click Add signature (or + New signature) and type a signature name (e.g. Standard).
  6. Click inside the signature editor box and paste your signature (Press Cmd+V on Mac or Ctrl+V on Windows).
  7. Under Select default signatures, set For New Messages and For Replies/Forwards to Standard.
  8. Click Save.

  1. Click the Copy Signature (Outlook Ready) button above.
  2. Open the Outlook App on your Mac.
  3. In the top menu bar, click Outlook → Settings... (or press Cmd + ,).
  4. Under Email, click Signatures.
  5. Click + (Add signature), name it Standard, and paste your copied signature into the box (Cmd+V).
  6. Set your default signature for New messages and Replies/forwards to Standard.
  7. Close the window to save.

  1. Click the Copy Signature (Outlook Ready) button above.
  2. Open Outlook Desktop App on Windows.
  3. Go to File → Options → Mail → Signatures... (or click New Email → Signature → Signatures...).
  4. Click New, type a signature name (e.g. Standard), and click OK.
  5. Click inside the signature edit box and paste your signature (Ctrl+V).
  6. Under Choose default signature, select your account and set New messages and Replies/forwards to Standard.
  7. Click OK to save.
